01

Mission

Artgenix’s aim is to preserve the folk art of Bangladesh, particularly rickshaw paint. This form of folk art is gradually nearing its extinction.

Presently, the rickshaws riding along the streets of Dhaka city barely have any colour on them when compared to how they were 2 decades ago. The artists who initially had produced these paintings in this sort of style did not have any education from art institutions, however, they had inherited it from either their ancestors or people surrounding them.

Unfortunately, the value of hand painted work reduced as digitalization had its rise in popularity. Therefore, the demand towards rickshaw paint is continually decreasing. Due to current circumstances, the number of original rickshaw artists are quite limited.

02

Vision

Our vision is to revive Bangladesh’s folk art.

We have a desire to portray the beauty of our endangered heritage through various forms of commonly used accessories such as home decoration, jewellery as well as clothing. We aim to bring light to this forgotten form of art and potentially have it be available to a worldwide audience.

Besides painting, we are also creating various forms of jewellery using resin, air dry clay, metal as well as fabric fusions. We’re also attempting to create opportunities for local artisans through our work.

03

Training Programs

For those aiming to cultivate their creativity, we include a month following post training practice sessions which are available both online and offline. Offline classes are located in Rampura, Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Supplies and materials will not be included, however, sources to buy them will be given by course instructors.
